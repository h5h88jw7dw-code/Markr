# T1.4 — The 1957 IMF drawings, from the Fund's side

### T1.4 — PARTIAL
**Route that worked:** Firecrawl scrape of FRUS 1955–57 Vol. VIII (South Asia), India chapter table of contents (`history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1955-57v08/ch6`) and Documents 166 and 167 directly. Cross-checked drawings figures against the IMF's own 1957 Annual Report (already captured for T0.1).

**Verbatim extract:**

Document 166 (Memorandum of a Conversation, Department of State, Washington, 31 May 1957 — the B.K. Nehru conversation already flagged in the brief as "already held"; now actually read in full):
> "India has already drawn $127½ million from the International Monetary Fund. Nevertheless, well before the end of this year Indian reserves will be reduced to their legal minimum. While India has the authority to go below this level temporarily, to do so would raise grave questions of the stability of its money."
> "The planned reduction of $400 million in reserves has already been exceeded, since India has used $650 million of reserves since April, 1956."

This independently corroborates (from the US State Department's own record of B.K. Nehru's own account) the $127.5 million drawing figure already found in the IMF's 1957 Annual Report (T0.1), and adds a load-bearing detail not previously sourced: **India's own Secretary of the Finance Ministry, in a private diplomatic conversation with no propaganda motive, stated that reserves were headed toward "their legal minimum"** — i.e. the statutory floor discussed in T1.7 — as of May 1957, independently confirming the "no remaining options" reading of the reserve position from the Indian side, not just the British or IMF side.

Document 167 (Ambassador Bunker to Bartlett "at London," 27 June 1957) — read in full; concerns US Congressional/World Bank/Export-Import Bank lobbying for aid to India. No mention of Britain's role in the IMF drawing, no mention of the drawing being "engineered" by British authorities, and "at London" refers only to Bartlett's onward posting, not to any British involvement in the drawing.

**Confidence tag:** STATED (the $127.5m figure and reserves-to-legal-minimum statement, both now independently corroborated from a non-British, non-Indian-government-propaganda source) / **OPEN, unresolved** (the specific claim — attributed throughout the brief to "Avaro" — that Britain *engineered* the 1957 drawings and *took the dollars*)
